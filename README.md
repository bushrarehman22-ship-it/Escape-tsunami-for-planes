# ✈️ Escape Tsunami for Planes 🌊

An adrenaline-pumping 3D Roblox-style survival, collection, and tycoon game built with Three.js!

Sprint along a long airport runway, rescue rare aircraft from incoming giant tsunamis, tow them safely back to your Airport Base hangar to generate massive passive $/second income, unlock upgrades, merge golden aircraft, and ascend through **Celestial Rebirths**!

---

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Sprint & Explore**: Venture down the endless runway track extending across 6 unique zones.
2. **Collect Aircraft**: Grab planes scattered along the runway (from Paper Planes and Spitfires to Concordes, F-22 Raptors, Space Shuttles, and Cosmic Dreadnoughts).
3. **Dodge Tsunamis**: Watch out for incoming tsunamis of varying speeds (Mild Waves, Crimson Tsunamis, Hyper Waves, and Cosmic Void Waves)!
4. **Take Cover in Bunkers**: Drop into deep trenches and underground shelters along the track so waves harmlessly wash over your head.
5. **Deposit & Earn**: Tow your rescued aircraft back to the safe Airport Base drop zone, parking them in your Hangar Pads to generate passive $/second income.
6. **Upgrade & Rebirth**: Purchase Sprint Thrusters, Cargo Tow Cables, Hangar Pad expansions, Magnet Radars, and perform Rebirths for massive permanent income multipliers and glowing Rebirth Wings!

## ⚡ Upgrades & Features

- **Sprint Jet Thrusters**: Increases running speed to outrun faster tsunamis.
- **Cargo Tow Cables**: Carry up to 10 aircraft at once with physical dynamic tow lines.
- **Hangar Parking Pads**: Expand base slots up to 34 parking pads.
- **Tractor Beam Magnet**: Auto-grabs aircraft from a wider radius.
- **Rocket Boost Dash**: Dash cooldown & burst distance.
- **Tsunami Warning Radar**: Real-time 2D mini-radar showing incoming waves, safe trenches, and spawned aircraft.
- **Golden Fusion Forge**: Merge 3 duplicate planes into a Golden Edition with 3x income!
- **Mystery Airdrop Crates**: Open Wooden, Steel, Military, and Quantum capsules for secret rare aircraft.
- **Web Audio API Synth**: Procedural roaring tsunamis, emergency sirens, chimes, cash sounds, and aviation synth music.
- **Mobile & Touch Ready**: Responsive virtual joystick & on-screen jump/dash controls.
